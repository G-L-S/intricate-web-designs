TIMELINE — CONSOLIDATION PATTERN (DRAFT v4)
=============================================

1996    Seed IP protections expanded under Plant Variety Protection Act amendments.
        First wave of utility patents on seed genetics.

1998    Monsanto acquires DeKalb Genetics ($2.3B) and Delta & Pine Land.
        Market share in cotton seed: 12% → 85% in under a decade.

2001    Four firms control 47% of North American seed corn market.

2005    "Technology use agreements" become standard. Farmers sign away right to
        save seed. Enforcement begins — private investigators in rural counties.

2008    Dow and DuPont begin cross-licensing. Effectively a single product line
        sold under two names.

2012    Syngenta and Monsanto settle patent dispute. Terms sealed.

2014    [REDACTED — THREE FOIA REQUESTS RETURNED FULLY REDACTED DOCUMENTS]
        [SEE: FOIA-2024-0041, FOIA-2024-0042, FOIA-2024-0043]

2016    Bayer-Monsanto merger announced. $66B. Largest agricultural acquisition
        in history.

2018    Four companies now control approximately 60% of global seed sales and
        75% of agrochemical sales.

2020    COVID supply chain disruptions expose single-source dependencies.
        Independent suppliers who might have provided alternatives no longer exist.

2023    State-level certification requirements begin appearing. Model legislation
        traced to industry working group. See: field_notes_midwest_q3.txt

2024    Present day. Three companies. One food system. No alternatives.

Note: The 2014 gap is not an oversight. Something happened that year that
connected the regulatory framework to the financial architecture. I have the
documents. They have the redactions.
